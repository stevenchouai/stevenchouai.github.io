Future

Where I'm heading and why. These aren't vague aspirations — they are concrete directions I actively invest in every single day. For the full timeline of my journey, see About.

Technology and code are vehicles; living deeply, healthily, and with agency is the destination. I want to build a life where I am in full alignment with my values, shedding external noise and unnecessary friction to focus on what genuinely matters.

  • Cultivate unwavering self-command: align daily habits with long-term compound growth
  • Reject performative hustle in favor of 'Useful Work' (有用功) that yields tangible leverage
  • Maintain inner calm and poise, building resilience regardless of external volatility
  • Invest generously in relationships, shared experiences, and mental clarity

The physical body is the ultimate hardware platform. Without vitality and physical confidence, cognitive ambitions are hollow. I treat physical conditioning as a non-negotiable daily craft.

  • Consistent strength training and progressive overload to sculpt a lean, athletic physique (薄肌)
  • Strict dietary and recovery hygiene to maintain single-digit or low-teen body fat and high daily energy
  • Translate the discipline of physical transformation into grounded confidence across all life domains
  • Treat health, sleep, and physical stamina as the highest-return long-term investments

I want to live in the cities I love, on my own schedule, without being tethered to a single desk or geography. By developing world-class async engineering skills, I aim to achieve true location independence.

  • Master distributed, asynchronous execution that delivers exceptional value from anywhere in the world
  • Experience life across global cities, immersing in diverse cultures, food, and creative communities
  • Cultivate a minimalist, portable lifestyle where tools and possessions fit in a backpack
  • Embrace exploration as a continuous source of fresh perspective and intellectual vitality

In engineering, I champion a full-stack ethos where engineers own what they build from end to end. Focusing on global payment rails and clearing systems, I aim to master mission-critical financial systems at scale.

  • Bridge feature engineering and test infrastructure: build it, test it, verify it, own it
  • Ensure zero-tolerance reliability and resilience across high-concurrency payment and settlement flows
  • Leverage AI agents and modern tooling to automate routine verification and amplify engineering leverage
  • Build durable engineering assets that compound value and operate cleanly under high traffic
